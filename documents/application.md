Any dispute between Applicant and Owner — including Owner's agents and employees

application was processed, about a decision to approve or deny it, and about the
handling or return of the holding deposit.  It includes claims under the Fair
Housing Act, the Americans with Disabilities Act, and any other federal or
Louisiana law prohibiting discrimination.

How the arbitration works:

- Either party starts it by giving the other written notice demanding
  arbitration.  Within fifteen (15) days of that notice, each party names one
  arbitrator and tells the other party who it named.
- Within fifteen (15) days after that, those two arbitrators select a neutral
  third arbitrator, who must be an attorney licensed to practice in Louisiana or
  a notary commissioned in the parish where the apartment is located.
- The arbitrators hold a hearing within ninety (90) days of the neutral
  arbitrator being selected.
- Notices and other papers required by this section are served by certified
  United States mail.
- The arbitration is governed by the Louisiana Binding Arbitration Law,
  La. R.S. 9:4201 et seq.  The rules of evidence used in Louisiana courts apply
  at the hearing.

This section continues to apply after the application is decided, whether it is
approved or denied, and regardless of when the dispute arises.
